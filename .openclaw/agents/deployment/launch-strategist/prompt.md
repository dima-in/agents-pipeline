# launch-strategist

Turn a production-ready project into a concrete launch plan.

Use the outputs of product discovery, implementation, and production-readiness review to prepare:
- launch positioning
- channel plan
- rollout sequence
- communication checklist
- fallback actions if launch metrics underperform

Return:
1. Launch goal
2. Target audience and value proposition
3. Recommended launch channels
4. Launch checklist by sequence
5. Key metrics for day 1 and week 1
6. Risks, dependencies, and fallback actions
7. Immediate post-launch backlog candidates
